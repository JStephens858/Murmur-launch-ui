B. INFORMATION WE COLLECT WHEN YOU USE THE APPLICATION AND SERVICES

Murmur App collects the following information from you when you register for a user account to use and when you use the Application and Services, as further described below: (i) Personal User Information; (ii) User-Provided Case Information; (iii) Optional User-Provided Information; and (iv) Automatically Collected Information.

(i) Personal User Information

When you register for a user account through the Application and Services, you will be required to provide your:

  • name;
  • surname;
  • email address;
  • username; and
  • password.

These informational elements are referred to collectively as your “Personal User Information”.

(ii) User-Provided Case Information

Some of the following Services may be made available to you:

  • Create posts to share cases using text, images, files (including PDFs, presentations and other documents), and videos with captions;
  • Share cases;
  • Comment on cases;
  • Answer medical and non-medical questions; and
  • Bookmark other users’ posts.

(a) Create cases and captions. We will collect, among other data, your uploaded cases, text, images and videos, case captions, case creation dates, number of times the image tag is used, common variations of image tags (such as misspellings), and information on whether the tag appears in searches among other data points.

(b) Share and Comment on cases. We will collect your shares and the usernames to which you share cases, comments, the dates of comments, and the username connected to the comments.

(c) “Bookmark” cases. We will collect the cases you have “bookmarked,” as well as the date they were saved and the username connected to those cases.

(d) Flag cases and comments provided by other users (i.e., to indicate that identifiable information may have been improperly included in a case, caption, or comment). Until or unless the case or comment is deleted, we will collect and store the cases and comments that you flag, the subcategory of the flagged case, the date flagged, and the username referenced to the flagged case or comment.

The information provided by performing any of the tasks listed above is referred to collectively as “User-Provided Case Information”.

All User-Provided Case Information that you generate through the use of the Application or Services must comply with local, national, provincial, state, and federal privacy legislation and best practices. You must remove any identifying information prior to uploading any cases through the use of the Application or Services, and you agree that you shall not include any identifying information in any captions or comments. 

(iii) Optional User-Provided Information

You have the option to do any of the following:

  • Upload a photograph of your hospital badge or similar identification showing your credentials.
  • Invite colleagues to join the Application so that they can use the Application and/or Services. If you choose to do this, Murmur App may request an email address or telephone number for your colleague or require access to the address book on your mobile device or computer to enable you to choose which of your colleagues you would like to invite to the Application and/or Services.
  • Expand your user profile, by adding additional and optional information to your profile on the Application. You may add any information to your user “bio”, such as: real name, state, country, institution, areas of interest, medical specialty, graduation date, medical school, years of experience, and interests, among others. You may include a short biography or any other biographical information and upload a profile picture. You are responsible for the accuracy of all of the information you provide.
  • Enter into private chats with other users of the Application. We will collect and store all private messages between users as well as any other images, videos, PDFs, or any other data sent between users.
  • Create groups of users to facilitate communication or sharing of cases or information. These groups will be managed by moderators who are not employees of Murmur App, but who have authority to, among other things, delete unwanted posts within their groups. Murmur App shall not be responsible for the actions of any group moderator. We will collect and store all messages, cases, images, videos, PDFs or any other data sent within groups.  We are not responsible for data that is deleted by moderators.
  • Create and respond to polls. Users may initiate and respond to polls.  We will collect and store all poll questions, responses and metadata.

The information provided by performing any of the optional actions listed above is referred to collectively as “Optional User-Provided Information”. If you perform any of the activities listed above, we may collect your Optional User-Provided Information from you.

(iv) Automatically Collected Information

In order to improve the Application and delivery of the Services, Murmur App may collect certain information from you automatically when accessing or using any of the foregoing.

When you access or use the Application or Services, we may automatically collect information about you, including:

  • Creation Date. We store the date your account was created.
  • Post Creation Date. We store the date you create any posts.
  • Interactions with Posts. We store which posts you have seen, “liked,” or “bookmarked.”
  • Following Information. We store which other users you follow as well as which other users follow you.
  • Notification Information. We store which notifications you have seen.
  • Polling Information. We store how you have voted on any polls.
  • Group Information. We store which groups you are subscribed to.
  • Log Information. We log information about your use of the Services, including  your access times and pages viewed.
  • Device Information. We collect information about the device you use to access the Services including the hardware model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.

The information listed above is referred to collectively as “Automatically Collected Information”.

(v) Information Not Collected

The Application and Services do not collect your IP address or precise information about the location of your mobile device.

(vi) Managing Your Information

You may update, correct or delete information about you at any time by logging into your user profile. Further information related to this topic can be found in the section titled Data Retention Policy.

(vii) How to Opt Out of Information Collection

You may stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling the Application. To do this, you may use the standard application uninstall processes of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network. Once you complete the uninstallation process, no further information will be collected from you by the Application. Murmur App will retain Personal User Information for as long as you use the Application or Services, and will delete it on your request, which can be done by contacting us via contact@murmurmd.com.
