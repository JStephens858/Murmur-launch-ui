B. Information We Collect

Murmur App collects the following information from you when you register for a user account to use and when you use the Services, as further described below: (i) Personal User Information; (ii) User-Provided Case Information; (iii) Optional User-Provided Information; and (iv) Automatically Collected Information.

(i)Personal User Information

When you register for a user account through the Services, you will be required to provide your:

  • name;
  • surname;
  • email address;
  • username; and
  • password.

These informational elements are referred to collectively as your “Personal User Information”.

(ii)User-Provided Case Information

Some of the following Services may be made available to you:

  • Create posts to share cases using text, images, files (including PDFs, presentations and other documents), and videos with captions;
  • Share cases;
  • Comment on cases;
  • Answer medical and non-medical questions;
  • Otherwise interact with us and other users on the Application; and
  • Bookmark other users’ posts.
(a)
Create cases and captions. We will collect, among other data, your uploaded cases, text, images and videos, case captions, case creation dates, number of times the image tag is used, common variations of image tags (such as misspellings), and information on whether the tag appears in searches among other data points.
(b)
Share and Comment on cases. We will collect your shares and the usernames to which you share cases, comments, the dates of comments, and the username connected to the comments.
(c)
“Bookmark” cases. We will collect the cases you have “bookmarked,” as well as the date they were saved and the username connected to those cases.
(d)
Flag cases and comments provided by other users (i.e., to indicate that identifiable information may have been improperly included in a case, caption, or comment). Until or unless the case or comment is deleted, we will collect and store the cases and comments that you flag, the subcategory of the flagged case, the date flagged, and the username referenced to the flagged case or comment.

The information provided by performing any of the tasks listed above is referred to collectively as “User-Provided Case Information”.

All User-Provided Case Information that you generate through the use of the Services must comply with local, national, provincial, state, and federal privacy legislation and best practices. You must remove any identifying information (including personal information and protected health information as well as any information capable of being associated with any individual or entity) prior to uploading any cases through the use of the Services, and you agree that you shall not include any identifying information in any captions or comments.

(iii)Optional User-Provided Information

You have the option to do any of the following:

  • Upload a photograph of your hospital badge or similar identification showing your credentials.
  • Provide your National Provider Identifier (“NPI”).
  • Invite colleagues to join the Application so that they can use the Services. If you choose to do this, Murmur App may request an email address or telephone number for your colleague or require access to the address book on your mobile device or computer to enable you to choose which of your colleagues you would like to invite to the Services. Do not provide us with any personal information relating to others unless you have the appropriate rights to do so.
  • Expand your user profile, by adding additional and optional information to your profile on the Application. You may add any information to your user “bio”, such as: real name, state, country, institution, areas of interest, medical specialty, graduation date, medical school, years of experience, and interests, among others. You may include a short biography or any other biographical information and upload a profile picture. You are responsible for the accuracy of all of the information you provide.
  • Enter into direct messages with other users of the Application. We will collect and store all direct messages between users as well as any other images, videos, PDFs, or any other data sent between users.
  • Create groups of users to facilitate communication or sharing of cases or information. These groups will be managed by moderators who are not employees of Murmur App, but who have authority to, among other things, delete unwanted posts within their groups. Murmur App shall not be responsible for the actions of any group moderator. We will collect and store all messages, cases, images, videos, PDFs or any other data sent within groups. We are not responsible for data that is deleted by moderators.
  • Create and respond to polls. Users may initiate and respond to polls. We will collect and store all poll questions, responses and metadata.

The information provided by performing any of the optional actions listed above is referred to collectively as “Optional User-Provided Information”. If you perform any of the activities listed above, we may collect your Optional User-Provided Information from you.

(iv)Automatically Collected Information

In order to improve the delivery of the Services, Murmur App may collect certain information from you automatically when accessing or using any of the foregoing. We will collect this information from all users of the Services, including any guests who are not logged into an account.

When you access or use the Services, we may automatically collect information about you, including:

  • Creation Date. We store the date your account was created.
  • Post Creation Date. We store the date you create any posts.
  • Interactions with Posts. We store which posts you have seen, “liked,” or “bookmarked.”
  • Following Information. We store which other users you follow as well as which other users follow you.
  • Notification Information. We store which notifications you have seen.
  • Polling Information. We store how you have voted on any polls.
  • Group Information. We store which groups you are subscribed to.
  • Log Information. We log information about your use of the Services, including your access times, actions taken, and pages viewed.
  • Usage Information. For example, we collect information about how users navigate the Murmur App, interact with our Services, and their actions when using the Services.
  • Device Information. We collect information about the device you use to access the Services including the hardware model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.

The information listed above is referred to collectively as “Automatically Collected Information”.

(v)Managing Your Information

You may update, correct or delete information about you at any time by logging into your user profile. Further information related to this topic can be found in the section titled Data Retention Policy.

(vi)How to Opt Out of Information Collection

You may stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling the Application. To do this, you may use the standard application uninstall processes of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network. Once you complete the uninstallation process, no further information will be collected from you by the Application. However, any information collected by Murmur App prior to uninstalling the Application may be retained for certain purposes – for further detail, please see the section titled Data Retention Policy.

You may also delete your account by sending an email to contact@murmurmd.com. If you request deletion, your profile information will be deleted from Murmur App’s server and your stored information will be dealt with in the manner described in the section titled Data Retention Policy.

(vii)Data Retention Policy

Murmur App will retain Personal User Information for as long as you use the Services, and will take steps to delete it on your request, which can be done by contacting us via contact@murmurmd.com. Your cases and comments will be anonymized and attributed to “Account Deleted.” You may request to have all images and comments associated with your account deleted. However, we cannot guarantee that we will be able to recall and delete images and comments that have been provided to third parties, such as medical journals or medical education websites. Please note that we may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

When we delete any information, it will be deleted from the active database, but may remain in Murmur App’s archives. We may also retain your information for fraud detection or similar purposes. All provisions of this Privacy Policy which by their nature should survive termination, shall survive termination, including without limitation, ownership provisions, warranty disclaimers, indemnity and limitations of liability.

If you contact Murmur App to delete your account, we will endeavor to process your request within seven (7) calendar days.
